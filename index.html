How the work is done

  • Primary records first

    Measured figures are traced to the filing, dataset or document they come from, and modelled or estimated inputs are identified as such.

  • Limits beside findings

    Each record states what its evidence cannot establish, from incomplete coverage to historical snapshots that are not current assessments.

  • AI-assisted, and disclosed

    AI models assist research, coding and drafting. Where a check must be independent, a model from a different lab reviews the work, and project records state model contributions and conflicts.

  • Versioned and archived

    Papers and datasets are archived on Zenodo with DOIs, and corrections are logged against the DOI when surfaced.
