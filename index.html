TOOL 01 LIVE BOARD

The AI Automation Claims Scorecard

A motive-neutral referee for the "AI writes most of our code" claims. It grades twelve vendor and adopter statements on four evidentiary tests: whether a denominator is stated, whether the figure is externally audited, whether an outsider could reproduce it, and whether a named party with no stake has checked that specific figure.
