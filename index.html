AI Data-Centre Water Tracker

An open, reproducible referee for data-centre water burden. It rebuilds the Water Consumption Impact index for ten sites from public data and self-checks each value against the source, then adds the two channels that framework leaves open: the hydropower coupling, and the off-site relocation of water that closed-loop cooling produces (which moves roughly 92 to 95 per cent of the footprint to the grid rather than removing it). Figures are bands and decompositions, and every input is named and motive-tagged.

The AI Research-Automation Scorecard

A motive-neutral referee for the "AI writes most of our code" claims. It grades eight vendor and adopter statements on four evidentiary tests: whether a denominator is stated, whether the figure is audited, whether it is reproducible, and whether a second source confirms it. It keeps a separate dated register of the labs' own research-loop red-lines, reported rather than scored, and reads as a complement to Chan et al. rather than a competitor.
