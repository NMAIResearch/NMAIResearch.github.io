The diagram shows a main route with return paths for rescoping and continuation. A study can revisit earlier stages and span several of the linked examples. Alignment means comparing the repository and deposit with the project-status record, not treating a methodological decision register as proof of agreement. Reframing or parking follows insufficient purpose, feasibility or evidence; a disconfirming finding is not a reason to suppress a result. The method allows descriptive work, replication and synthesis. The novelty stage is not a claim that every worthwhile study must discover something new.

No comparative workflow baseline, new source survey, fresh model experiment or full Lifecycle run was performed for this map. The script reproduces the presentation only. The author's choices and model/script assistance are distinct in the guide's disclosure.
