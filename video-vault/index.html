Same brief, different models

Clips made from the same written prompt by different video models, one try per model and shown as generated. They are not controlled tests: NM AI Research chose the shots, and the sizes and lengths differ as noted. The clips play muted.

Camel crossing. One prompt and one start frame, a frame from an earlier Caravan clip, given to Omni 1.1 Flash and Veo 3.1 Quality in Google Flow. Both clips are 1280 by 720 at 24 fps; Omni made 10 s and Veo 8 s.

The prompt

Side-on tracking shot at walking pace, camera level with the riders: a line of camel and rider silhouettes against a star-filled sky, lanterns swinging gently with each step. Context: the crest of a long dune. Night desert under a deep navy sky dense with stars, the Milky Way a soft pale band rising from lower left to upper right, no moon, no glow on the horizon. The only warm light comes from round cream paper lanterns with thin horizontal ribs and a small dark cap, always lit with a steady amber glow, hanging from the saddles. Single-humped camels walk forward at a steady pace, heads leading, all travelling from left to right across the frame. Riders wear dark indigo robes and head wraps and are seen only as silhouettes; no faces, no people on foot. Colours: indigo, navy and amber only. Smooth, slow cinematic camera, 16:9, full frame with no black bars, no vignette, no fisheye. A single continuous shot, no scene cuts, no text. Each camel keeps four legs that stay visible and move in a natural walking gait.
