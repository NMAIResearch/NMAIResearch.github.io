Research architecture behind the portfolio

Research continues around a loop. Questions wait in a queue; one is selected and scoped; evidence, analysis and challenge repeat; a version is published; its repository, deposit and project records are brought into agreement; monitoring or reuse can lead to a revision or a new question. A study can span several stages. A source catalogue, calculation or release record illustrates one step within it.

1. Question queue

Keep candidate questions with the date and reason they were raised. An entry is a lead, not a commitment. No public queue-entry example is linked here.

2. Selection and scope

Choose a question the available evidence can address; define its unit, population, period and comparison, and select a method suited to the question; check existing work and practical purpose. A contribution, useful replication or synthesis can justify proceeding. Otherwise narrow, reframe or archive.

3. Research and challenge

Collect records, preserve provenance and check reporting periods. Retain missing and conflicting evidence. Analyse with inspectable assumptions and quantities, test sensitivity where appropriate, seek counter-evidence, test alternative explanations, examine source support and revise claims that exceed the evidence. Return to scope when the question needs changing.

8. Archive branches

A question or study can pause, stop, be reframed or merge into other work. Retain the recorded decision and any useful materials. An archived item has not necessarily failed novelty. Missing history stays unrecorded.

Verification. The shared structure is the author’s description, supported by method documents and selected project records. Each link illustrates a step or supporting component; it does not certify that a study recorded every stage, used the same software or passed every gate. Missing history is left unrecorded. This map does not revalidate findings. Project-specific reproduction and review limits still apply. No workflow-effectiveness or time-saving baseline was run.
