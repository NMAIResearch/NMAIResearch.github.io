Research kit

AI Research Starter

For anyone interested in AI research: a starter pack to help you and your AI assistant plan, investigate, challenge and document a project that others can check. This is a public pilot edition, shared for trial and feedback. It is not evidence that the pack improves research quality.

01Download

AI_Research_Starter.zip (222 kB, 47 files, edition 2026-10-16.1-public-pilot).

Unzip it, read README.md, then fill in CLAUDE_START.md and give its prompt to your assistant. The prompt also works with other assistants.

To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with ef3e374781c47980016b1578d79294c37a4488d482d702b5c9dbed9e54a2496f.

02What is inside

  • A starting prompt with project details to fill in (CLAUDE_START.md).
  • Short working rules, the research workflow, and a method menu with short route notes (WORKING_RULES.md, WORKFLOW.md, METHOD_SELECTION.md and methods/).
  • Guides for checking prior work, sources and reproducible calculation, analysis checks, an optional separate review and a fair note on AI help (IDEACHECK.md, SOURCE_AND_REPRODUCTION.md, ANALYSIS_CHECKS.md, CROSS_REVIEW.md, AI_DISCLOSURE.md).
  • A project notebook for scope, evidence, decisions and what was done (PROJECT_NOTEBOOK.md).
  • Two small checkers with their tests: prosecheck.py looks for configured house-style and internal-marker patterns, and does not detect facts or authorship; vibecode_gate.py runs bounded Python and JSON checks.
  • A worked example: an earlier, unchanged version of our AI infrastructure paper with its reproduction script (examples/ai-infrastructure-v7.1/).

03What you need

An AI assistant, and Python 3 for the checkers and the example; they use only the standard library. The pack's Windows instructions have not yet been tried on Windows.

05Limits

Following the instructions or passing the scripts does not show that a source supports a claim, or that the research is correct. You own the substantive decisions and any release, and you follow any research-ethics, data-use or other rules that apply to your work. The pack does not replace advice from a subject specialist.

06Feedback

Observations from using the pack are welcome at NMAIResearch@proton.me. Please remove private work, personal data and credentials first. How email to that address is handled is on the privacy page.
