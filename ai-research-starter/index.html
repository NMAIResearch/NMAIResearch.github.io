AI Research Starter

AI Research Starter is a public pilot pack from NM AI Research for planning, investigating, challenging and documenting a checkable project with an AI assistant. It is for anyone interested in AI research, including students, researchers and practitioners, and people reviewing their work. It provides research guidance, a starting prompt, a project notebook, small checkers and a historical reproduction example. It is shared for trial and feedback. This pack is not evidence of improved research quality.

01Download

AI_Research_Starter.zip (222,606 bytes, 47 files, edition 2026-10-17.1-public-pilot).

Unzip the pack and read the contents table and quick start in README.md. Fill in the project details in START_HERE.md, give its prompt and the permitted materials to your assistant, and agree a small initial plan. The prompt works with other assistants too.

To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with 0cf86a97588f786516420eabce656dd156c5cb99c93de199a83f893f0aa9116c.

02What is inside

  • A starting prompt with project details to fill in (START_HERE.md).
  • Short working rules, the research workflow, and a method menu with short route notes (WORKING_RULES.md, WORKFLOW.md, METHOD_SELECTION.md and methods/).
  • Guides for checking prior work, sources and reproducible calculation, analysis checks, an optional separate review and a fair note on AI help (IDEACHECK.md, SOURCE_AND_REPRODUCTION.md, ANALYSIS_CHECKS.md, CROSS_REVIEW.md, AI_DISCLOSURE.md).
  • A project notebook for scope, evidence, decisions and what was done (PROJECT_NOTEBOOK.md).
  • Two small checkers with their tests: style_check.py looks for configured house-style and internal-marker patterns, and does not detect facts or authorship; code_gate.py runs bounded Python and JSON checks. CODE_FAILURE_GATES.md describes the project-specific checks that the static gate does not implement.
  • A worked example: an earlier, unchanged version of our AI infrastructure paper with its reproduction script (examples/ai-infrastructure-v7.1/).

03What you need

An AI assistant, and Python 3 for the checkers and the example; they use only the standard library. On 17 October 2026 its commands ran without errors on Windows, macOS and Ubuntu.

05Limits

Following the instructions or passing the scripts does not show that a source supports a claim, or that the research is correct. You own the substantive decisions and any release, and you follow any research-ethics, data-use or other rules that apply to your work. The pack does not replace advice from a subject specialist.

06Feedback

Observations from using the pack are welcome at NMAIResearch@proton.me. Please remove private work, personal data and credentials first. How email to that address is handled is on the privacy page.
